Get Your Pass

Pass and entry rules from Xpressions’25

  • Register separately for Day 1 and Day 2. Registrations beyond capacity are waitlisted.
  • Passes are not sold. They are issued through registration, one per ID, to your registered email.
  • Single entry only. No re-entry after leaving the venue.
  • XIMB and XIM University students enter with their college ID.
  • External attendees must register to receive an entry pass.
  • Comedy Night is first come, first served. EDM Night is open to registered attendees.
  • Registration implies consent to photography and videography for promotion.

The 2027 pass policy will be confirmed by the organising team and updated here.

FAQs

Is Xpressions only for management students?

No. It combines business competitions with music, dance, theatre, fashion, comedy and concerts, so there is something for every kind of talent.

Are passes free or paid?

In 2025, passes were not for sale and were issued through registration. Check back here for the confirmed 2027 policy.

Do XIM University students need a pass?

In 2025, XIMB and XIM University students entered with their college ID.

Can I re-enter after leaving the venue?

In 2025 entry was single only, with no re-entry.

When and where is the fest?

The probable dates are 21–23 January 2027. Final dates and venue details will be confirmed by the organising team.
